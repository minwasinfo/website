Our Process

How We Turn Plastic
Into Fuel

Our continuous-feed pyrolysis plant runs 24/7, converting mixed plastic waste into usable fuel oil, carbon black, and gas with zero liquid discharge.

1
Collection
We receive or collect plastic waste from suppliers. Material is weighed, logged, and documented.
2
Pre-treatment
Plastic is shredded, moisture-dried, and sorted to remove PVC and non-processable materials.
3
Pyrolysis
Shredded plastic is heated to 400–500°C in a sealed, oxygen-free reactor. Polymer chains crack into hydrocarbons.
4
Condensation
Hot vapours pass through a condenser, liquefying into pyrolysis oil. Non-condensable gas is recycled as burner fuel.
5
Dispatch
Fuel oil and carbon black are stored and dispatched to buyers. Flue gas is scrubbed before release.

Carbon Black

Recovered carbon black (rCB) with calorific value ~7,000 kcal/kg. Used as a low-cost fuel in kilns or as a filler/reinforcing agent in rubber and plastic manufacturing.

Best Used In

Combustible Gas
Self-used internally

Non-condensable pyrolysis gas (~8,000 kcal/m³) generated during the process is recycled directly as fuel for our reactor burners — keeping our operating costs low and reducing diesel consumption.

⚠ Not Accepted

We currently do not accept: PVC / polyvinyl chloride, PET bottles (better value in mechanical recycling), medical or biohazardous plastic waste, or heavily contaminated material mixed with glass or metal. When in doubt, contact us before dispatching.
